## How to Read This Doctrine

This document is a structural doctrine, not a technical specification, policy proposal, or ethics framework. It does not prescribe how AI systems must be built, measured, optimized, or enforced. Instead, it defines non-negotiable constraints on behavior—specifically, how systems may represent knowledge, uncertainty, and authority without misleading users. The doctrine is intentionally agnostic to implementation, metrics, and architecture; disagreement with its premises does not invalidate it, but claiming compliance while violating its constraints does. Readers should treat this text as a constitutional boundary: it names what must never happen silently, while leaving all solutions, mechanisms, and trade-offs to competing implementations and future evolution.
